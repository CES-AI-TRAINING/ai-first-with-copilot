# Chain-of-Thought (CoT)

Description: Ask the model to show intermediate reasoning steps to improve correctness on complex tasks.

Prompt:

```
System: Explain your thinking step-by-step, then provide the final concise answer.
User: A store has 12 apples. If 3 customers each buy 2 apples, how many apples remain?
```

Expected output (example):

"Step 1: 3 customers × 2 apples = 6 apples sold.
Step 2: 12 - 6 = 6 apples remain.
Answer: 6"
